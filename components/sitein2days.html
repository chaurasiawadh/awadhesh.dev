About

sitein2days.online is my own freelance venture, a digital studio I am building to take on client projects end-to-end. The goal is simple: help businesses move faster with practical AI, e-commerce, and data solutions without the overhead of a large agency.

I started this to work directly with founders and teams who need reliable delivery: websites, Shopify stores, AI automation, and analytics, packaged in a clear, outcome-focused engagement model.

What I Offer

  • Custom AI Agents & Automation: Workflow automation, chatbots, and intelligent agents that cut repetitive work and keep customers engaged 24/7.
  • Shopify Development & E-commerce: Storefronts, headless commerce, app integrations, and conversion-focused shopping experiences.
  • Data Analytics & Power BI: Dashboards, pipelines, and business intelligence that turn raw data into decisions teams can act on.
  • Full-stack product delivery: From idea and architecture to launch and iteration, built for speed, quality, and maintainability.

Why This Exists

  • A personal brand for taking freelance and contract work with clear ownership.
  • One place for clients to discover services across AI, Shopify, and analytics.
  • Built to scale from small launches to production-ready digital systems.
