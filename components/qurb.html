About

Qurb is a Muslim marriage app built to help people find meaningful matches with a focused, respectful, and modern mobile experience on both Android and iOS.

As the lead freelance engineer on the project, I worked across performance, release automation, and product polish - taking the app from active development through Play Store and App Store launch.

What I Built

  • App performance: Optimized startup and screen loading with profiling, lazy loading, and leaner render paths.
  • CI/CD with Fastlane: Automated Android and iOS builds, signing, and store-ready deploy workflows.
  • Release engineering: Streamlined beta and production releases for faster, more reliable shipping.
  • Product quality: Improved onboarding, matching flows, and overall app stability ahead of launch.
  • Store delivery: Shipped production builds to Google Play and the Apple App Store.
